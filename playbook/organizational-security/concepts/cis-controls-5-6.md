# CIS Controls 5 and 6: Account and Access Control Management

## Overview
CIS Control 5 focuses on managing user, administrator, and service accounts. CIS Control 6 focuses on granting, reviewing, and removing access to systems and data.

## Key Concepts
- **Account management:** Track accounts from creation through removal.
- **Least privilege:** Give users only the permissions needed for their work.
- **Multifactor authentication:** Require additional verification beyond a password.
- **Access reviews:** Regularly check whether permissions are still appropriate.

## Why It Matters
Inactive accounts and unnecessary permissions create opportunities for unauthorized access. Managing accounts and permissions helps protect sensitive information and limit the damage from compromised credentials.

## Common Uses
- Creating accounts for new employees.
- Updating permissions when job responsibilities change.
- Disabling accounts when employees leave.
- Reviewing account access during security assessments.

## Best Practices
- Maintain an inventory of user, administrator, and service accounts.
- Disable unnecessary or inactive accounts.
- Use separate accounts for administrative tasks.
- Require multifactor authentication where applicable.
- Regularly review permissions and remove unnecessary access.
